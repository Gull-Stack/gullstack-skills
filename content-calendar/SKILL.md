# SKILL: Organic content & social calendar

How GullStack runs organic content for local clients. Written 2026-08.
**[load-bearing]** = the system breaks without it; *[approx.]* = benchmark.
Doctrine: **organic is the proving ground, paid is the amplifier** (meta-ads §5:
post organic first, put spend behind proven winners). Organic also feeds AEO —
real photos/videos are the evidence layer answer engines and customers both read
(seo-master). This skill extracts the content guidance embedded in meta-ads and
retail-resale into one standalone system any client vertical can run.

## 1. Content pillars (per client, at onboarding)

Pick 3–4 pillars; every post belongs to one. The standard local-business set:
1. **Proof** — results, before/after, payouts, happy customers (with permission),
   reviews-as-content. The conversion pillar.
2. **Product/drop** — what's new/available right now (merchandising-as-content,
   retail-resale §loop: the rack IS the content). The urgency pillar.
3. **People/behind-the-scenes** — staff on camera, process, the shop's day. The
   trust pillar; UGC-style beats polished (meta-ads §5).
4. **Educational/answers** — the questions customers actually ask (mine the
   seo-master intent map; each strong one is also an answer-hub candidate). The
   authority pillar.
Villains stay concrete (no icon-grid abstractions — the imagery doctrine applies
to feeds too: real photos, real numbers, real people).

## 2. Minimum viable cadence **[load-bearing]**

The floor that must survive busy weeks — consistency beats bursts:
- **3 posts/week**: 1 Reel (9:16, hook in 2–3s, 7–30s, captions — the meta-ads §5
  spec), 1 proof post, 1 product/drop post.
- **1 GBP post/week** + new GBP photos weekly (GBP is the highest-intent surface a
  local business has — retail-resale doctrine).
- **1 story-tier touch/day** *[approx.]* where the platform matters (IG stories) —
  low-production, reuse the day's shot.
Anything above the floor is bonus; below it for 2+ weeks, the system is broken —
fix production (§4), don't exhort harder.

## 3. Hooks (the first 2 seconds are the post)

Hook formulas that work for local (rotate, don't repeat weekly):
"We paid $X cash for this today" (number-led proof) · "POV: you..." (viewer-cast) ·
"Don't [common action] until you see this" (pattern interrupt) · "Everything new
in the store this week" (drop tour) · "Answering the #1 question we get" (educational)
· before/after cold-open (no intro, result first).
Text-on-screen restates the hook; keep it out of UI safe zones. One CTA per post,
directive (three-kings close rules apply to captions too).

## 4. Production: batch monthly, shoot once, use five times

- **Monthly batch session** (60–90 min on-site): shoot against next month's theme
  from the seasonality calendar (retail-resale §seasonality) + a shot list built
  from the pillars. Staff shoot phone-vertical; imperfect is on-brand.
- **The repurposing chain [load-bearing]** — one shoot feeds every surface:
  Reel → cutdown to story → stills to GBP photos + weekly GBP post → best frame
  into the email/SMS campaign (email-sms-lifecycle §4) → proof shots into the
  website/answer hubs (site-builder/seo-master) → winners get ad spend (meta-ads).
  Nothing is shot for only one surface.
- Approval loop: client owner approves the monthly calendar once, not every post
  — pre-approval kills cadence.

## 5. Calendar ops

One rolling doc per client (calendar lives with the client's marketing docs, saved
per the GitHub+Notion protocol): month theme → weekly slots (pillar, hook, asset,
surface, CTA, UTM per analytics-reporting §5) → status. Fill next month during
this month's batch session. Seasonal peaks get planned 4–8 weeks out (the same
lead time as the paid surge in meta-ads' resale example — organic and paid run
the same calendar).

## 6. Measurement

- **Saves + shares > likes** — they predict reach and signal keep-worthy content.
  Watch completion rate on Reels; a strong hook shows in the 3-second retention.
- Winners (top ~10% engagement) → "existing post" ad spend (meta-ads §5), the
  organic→paid pipeline that is the point of the whole system.
- Monthly report line (analytics-reporting §6): posts shipped vs cadence floor,
  top post + why, follower-to-list conversion (bio link tagged — the feed is
  rented, the list is owned: bryce gate #5).

## 7. Mistakes (enforce as review checklist)

Posting only when there's "news" (cadence dies) · polished-agency look replacing
phone-shot UGC · hooks buried behind logos/intros · every post a sales pitch
(pillar balance) · shooting for one surface only · GBP ignored while IG gets
everything · engagement-bait unrelated to the business · no permission for
customer-visible shots · winners never boosted · calendar in someone's head
instead of the doc.

## Worked example: Plato's Closet Draper

Pillars: proof ("we paid $47 cash for this bag today"), drop ("Fresh Finds rack
tour — everything new this week" synced to the physical rack), people (staff
styling picks), answers ("what we're buying this season"). Batch shoot = rack
restock day. Chain: rack-tour Reel → GBP photos → buy-side alert email image →
best Reel gets Engagement spend. Sell-side proof posts are the unfair advantage —
nobody else in the market shows cash-in-hand.
