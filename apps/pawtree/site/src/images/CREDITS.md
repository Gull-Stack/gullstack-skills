## 🔴 These are stock. Swap priority before launch.

The house rule is *real photos > stock > none*, and **never launch with stock in
the hero**. Stock is here because it beats grey placeholder boxes for a client
preview — it is NOT the launch state.

1. **`00-hero-dog-HERO.jpg` — highest priority.** The hero must be the client's
   own dog. Everything else can wait; this one is the rule.
2. **`author-portrait.jpg`** — must be her, with her dogs. It sits on the About
   page under her bio; a stranger there undercuts the whole E-E-A-T argument the
   site makes.
3. Guide covers 01–06 — fine as stock for launch, better as her dogs over time.

Replacing is a filename swap: keep the same names and dimensions and nothing else
needs to change. Target ~2000px wide for the hero, ~900px for covers.
